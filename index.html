How it works

Four automated steps, three outcomes. Reviewers only touch the middle one.

1

Seller uploads

The video lands in Best Buy’s Cloudinary cloud through the marketplace feed

2

Virus scan

Perception Point clears the file before anything else runs

3

MediaFlows prepares

Checks for audio, generates the transcript, hands the video to moderation

4

Moderation runs

30+ checks on the narration, the on-screen text and the frames

every video gets one of three outcomes

Auto-approved

Confident pass on every check. No human involved.

Needs review

The model is not sure. A reviewer decides in the queue, with the flagged moment pointed out.

Rejected

Confident fail, with the reason and the timestamp where it was found.
